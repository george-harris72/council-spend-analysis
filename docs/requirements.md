# Requirements Document defined for Council Spend Analysis reporting project

## Background
Westminster City Council, spend data published under the transparency code, and the request you've been given (something like "can we get a monthly view of where our money's going?").
## Purpose
Supporting the monthly finance review.
## Audience and cadence
The Section 151 officer and service directors, monthly, short read.
## Decisions supported
Three or four questions they'll bring. Which service areas are spending ahead of last year? Which suppliers are we dependent on? What's new or changed?
## Assumptions
Numbered, challengeable.
## In scope
Numbered requirements, each tracing to a decision.
## Out of scope, with reasons
No budget data, no commitments or accruals, sub-£500 invisible, redacted payments.
## Open questions with recommendations
e.g. how to treat credit notes, and whether redacted payments count in totals.
## Known data quality constraints
Leave this mostly empty; you fill it in at Step 5 from real profiling.
